The problem

Scams Are Easier to Encounter Than You Think

📩

Suspicious Messages

People receive messages that may look legitimate but contain scam patterns.

🔗

Suspicious Links

Users may click unfamiliar links without knowing where they lead.

💼

Fake Opportunities

Job offers, investments, prizes, and other opportunities can be used to deceive people.

💰

Financial Loss

Scams can lead to financial loss or stolen personal information.

🔄

Repeated Scams

People may encounter scams that have already affected others without knowing about previous reports.

The problem isn’t only detecting scams. It’s knowing what to look for before making a decision.

    Safety Tips

    Stay One Step Ahead of Scammers

    🔐

    Protect Your Information

    Never share passwords, OTPs, or sensitive information with unknown people.

    💳

    Think Before You Pay

    Be careful when someone asks for upfront payment or urgent transfers.

    🔗

    Check Before You Click

    Don’t blindly open unfamiliar links — check where they really lead first.

    ⏰

    Don’t Let Urgency Decide

    Scammers often pressure people to act immediately. Take a moment to think it through.

    🔎

    Verify First

    Check information through official sources before trusting an offer.
